Fair questions, straight answers

Do job seekers ever pay?

Never. Not for applying, not for "premium profile", not for seeing salaries. Employers fund the platform because hiring is worth money to them; looking for work should never cost you.

What happens if I use my posts before the month ends?

On Free, you wait for the next month or upgrade. On Standard, you can top up a single post for $9 any time — no forced upgrades.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, one click, no retention call. Your live listings stay up until their period ends, and annual plans are refunded pro-rata for unused months.

How does the verified badge work?

We check that the company is real (registration number or domain + a human review call). It takes about a day. Verified listings get roughly three times more applications.

Do you discount for nonprofits or first hires?

Nonprofits and schools get 40% off Standard, forever. A company's very first hire through Canopy is free on any plan — the first one should feel effortless.

What payment methods do you take?

Card via Stripe, plus Paystack and Flutterwave for African markets, and PayPal. Invoicing is available on Premium annual.
